## The product

Ghal Bol is an encrypted 1:1 messenger. Chat history stays on devices. A delivery server holds temporary ciphertext it cannot read. Calls are designed to travel peer-to-peer or through an opaque coordination bridge. There is no phone-number account and no cloud chat archive operated by us.
